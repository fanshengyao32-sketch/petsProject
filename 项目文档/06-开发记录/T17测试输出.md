## 真实模拟渠道联调

正常成功及重复通知：

```text
booking=confirmed  payment=succeeded  payment_notification_records=2
```

其中两条记录分别为同步渠道结果和一个唯一回调通知；模拟渠道第二次发送相同通知号被数据库幂等忽略。

关闭后晚到成功：

```text
booking=closed  payment=succeeded  refund=pending  reason=late_payment
notification_records=1  audit_records=1
```

失败后重试成功、旧支付再成功：

```text
booking=confirmed  succeeded_payment_facts=2  refund=pending  reason=duplicate_payment
```
